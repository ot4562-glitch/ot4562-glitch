> Get more from what you already have.

I build systems that recover useful capability from existing hardware and AI before asking for a bigger upgrade, a larger model, or a more expensive stack.

## Resource amplification

### HydraSeat

Turn one sufficiently capable Windows PC into two local gaming stations, with separate display, input/controller, audio, and game-control paths where the platform allows it.

https://github.com/ot4562-glitch/HydraSeat

### ExactScope

A thin AI capability amplifier for getting more useful performance or lower inference cost from an AI stack you already have.

ExactScope research is developed in public. Positive, negative, and inconclusive results are kept when they are reproducible and legally publishable.

https://github.com/ot4562-glitch/ExactScope

## What I care about

- capability per cost, token, byte, watt, and machine
- local and edge AI that does more before requiring a larger model
- systems software that reuses existing hardware instead of assuming another device
- reproducible experiments, including failed ideas rather than only successful demos
- small, reversible layers that justify their own overhead
